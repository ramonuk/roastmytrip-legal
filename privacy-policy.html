RoastMyTrip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

RoastMyTrip ("the App") is a mobile application that lets you upload a vacation photo and receive a humorous, AI-generated commentary ("roast") about it. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Photos you upload. When you choose or take a photo to roast, that image is sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini) for the sole purpose of generating your roast text and tourist score. We do not permanently store your photos on our servers.

Roast history and preferences. Your generated roasts, tourist scores, and app preferences (such as default roast intensity, photo quality, and sharing settings) are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or sync this data to any external server or account.

No account required. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any other personal identifying information.

2. How Your Photo Is Used

Photos you upload are sent securely to Google's Gemini AI model to analyze the image and generate a text-based roast and a "Tourist Score." The photo is processed for this single purpose and is not used to train AI models, is not shared with advertisers, and is not stored by us after processing.

3. Data Storage

All roast history, gallery images, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you delete the App or use the "Log out" feature within the App, this locally stored data is permanently cleared and cannot be recovered.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the Google Gemini API for AI-powered photo analysis and roast generation. We do not use advertising networks, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Your Choices

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your roast history and reset your preferences at any time using the "Log out" option in the App, or by deleting the App entirely.
